Before you get in touch

The things people ask first.

If your question is not here, ask it in the form — we answer every enquiry.

What does a quote cost?

Nothing. Quotes are free and carry no obligation. You will get it in writing, itemised, so you can compare it with anyone else.

Do you take one-off jobs?

Yes. A single end-of-tenancy clean or one repair is as welcome as a full management instruction.

How fast can you get to an urgent issue?

Urgent problems — leaks, electrical faults, security — should be phoned through rather than logged online, so we can respond straight away.

Is a snagging report the same as a survey?

No. Ours is an independent visual inspection and condition report designed to highlight visible defects and general condition concerns before purchase. It is not a structural survey or a valuation.

Do you work with tenants directly?

For properties we manage, yes — tenants report issues through the maintenance portal using the password we issue with the tenancy.

Are you insured?

Yes. Certificates are available on request before any work starts.
